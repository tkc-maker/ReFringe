Help for the Admin Proposal Editor:

Welcome to the ReFringe.org Admin Proposal Editor. Please follow the guidelines below:

  • Select (check) all Question Classes pertinent to the proposal. Then click Show Selected Questions/Answers. This will save your Proposal Title and Question Class selections. It will also reveal the questions that you have selected. Answer as many as possible. If we have further questions, like scheduling, we will contact you.
  • Questions marked with * are required. But, if you can't answer them at this time, enter "N/A" to bypass the requirement.
  • Some fields require a standard format, like email and phone. Should you enter the wrong format, a red message will appear above the field when you click the Save Answers button. Just correct your entries, and click the button again to save.
  • The Save Answers button appears above and below the list. As soon as you start to answer a question, these buttons will turn red, meaning that your entries have not been saved yet.
  • IMPORTANT: After answering questions, if you uncheck any Question Classes and then resave your selections, by clicking, Show Selected Questions/Answers, some of your answers may be lost (those corresponding to the Question Classes that you unchecked).
  • IMPORTANT: If you mistakenly unchecked Question Classes, and would like to see them checked again, please refresh the page.
  • Note: Above the Save Answers buttons, you will see text in red, "Do Validation?", followed by a checkbox. If checked, then fields like Phone and Email may require a specific format, e.g.: abc-def-ghij (numbers and dashes) or abc@defghijkl.mnp (don't forget the "@" and the "."). The checkbox is checked by default, but you may uncheck to bypass all validation (not recommended).
  • Note: To the right of Show Selected Questions/Answers, there are two buttons: Select A Venue and Plan Event. These lead to utilities for locating and scheduling the event.
