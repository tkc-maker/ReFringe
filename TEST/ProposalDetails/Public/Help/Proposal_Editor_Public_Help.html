Help for the Public Proposal Editor:

Welcome to the ReFringe.org Public Proposal Editor. Please follow the guidelines below:

  • The line reading, Click to Toggle Question Classes, will reveal or collapse the list of possible Question Classes when clicked.
  • Note: When the list is showing, the Proposal Title field becomes editable. And when the list is not showing, the Title field is disabled.
  • Select (check) all Question Classes pertinent to your proposal. Then click Show Selected Questions/Answers. This will save your Proposal Title and Question Class selections. It will also reveal the questions that you have selected. Answer as many as possible. If we have further questions, like scheduling, we will contact you.
  • Questions marked with * are required. But, if you can't answer them at this time, enter "N/A" to bypass the requirement.
  • Some fields require a standard format, like email and phone. Should you enter the wrong format, a red message will appear above the field when you click the Save Answers button. Just correct your entries, and click the button again to save.
  • The Save Answers button appears above and below the list. As soon as you start to answer a question, these buttons will turn red, meaning that your entries have not been saved yet.
  • IMPORTANT: After answering questions, if you uncheck any Question Classes and then resave your selections, by clicking, Show Selected Questions/Answers, some of your answers may be lost (those corresponding to the Question Classes that you unchecked).
  • IMPORTANT: If you mistakenly unchecked Question Classes, and would like to see them checked again, please refresh the page.
  • Note: Above the Save Answers buttons, you will see text in red, "Do Answer Validation?", followed by a checkbox. If checked, then fields like Phone and Email may require a specific format, e.g.: abc-def-ghij (numbers and dashes) or abc@defghijkl.mnp (don't forget the "@" and the "."). The checkbox is checked by default, but you may uncheck to bypass all validation, including field requirements (not recommended).r
